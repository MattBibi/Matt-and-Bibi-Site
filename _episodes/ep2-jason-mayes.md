## About This Episode

Jason Mayes, Web AI Lead at Google, joins hosts Matt & Bibi to talk about one of the most exciting developments in the web platform — AI that runs entirely in the browser, on your device, without sending data to a server.

## Topics We Cover

- Running ML models client-side with TensorFlow.js and MediaPipe
- Why on-device AI matters for privacy, latency, and offline experiences
- Getting started with Web AI as a web developer
- Real-world demos pushing the limits of the browser
- What's next for AI on the web platform
